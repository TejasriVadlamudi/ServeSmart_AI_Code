# Website handoff

The app-facing handoff in `data/forecasting/` contains clearly labeled historical May backtest CSV files from the recovered `64138acf` models: hourly Random Forest and daily XGBoost. They are not live forecasts for tomorrow and do not estimate people, wait times or attendance. The exact common-key verification is recorded in `Recovered_Model_Verification.json`; the older 37.37% and 22.54% figures are excluded. For future dates without the old model’s required feature snapshot, the Streamlit app uses a clearly labeled historical fallback.
